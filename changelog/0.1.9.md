# launchme 0.1.9

Released 2026-10-26.

## New

- **Uninstall in every game menu** (TV mode: the game's menu, PSfive's "…" included, and
  Details → "…"; the desktop window: right click and the pane's "…"): *Uninstall* is the last item, in red,
  for installed games Launchme knows how to remove. Steam games open Steam's own dialog. GOG games are
  removed silently by their own uninstaller after Launchme asks (*Uninstall* / *Open game folder* /
  *Cancel*; Windows asks for permission when the game needs it); a toast says when it is done. EA, Ubisoft,
  Battle.net, Amazon and other installers run the uninstaller they registered in Windows, Xbox games open
  Windows' Apps settings, games without one open their store's client. Never shown while the game runs.

## Fixes

- Details → "…" in TV mode drew fewer rows than it had: *Launch options* and *Uninstall* were missing
  from the menu although the pad could reach them.
- Long plugin descriptions ran into their tags (Settings → Plugins): the row now grows to hold up to three
  lines above the tags.
- Long paths in Settings → Library → DRM-free → *Found games* ran out of their thin rows (TV mode and the
  desktop window): they end with an ellipsis on one line.

## Changes

- **Controllers with Bluetooth off**: paired Bluetooth pads stay in Settings → Controllers and the
  Controllers screen, greyed, with "Bluetooth · off"; their *Settings* button opens Windows' Bluetooth
  settings. Before, they disappeared until Bluetooth was switched on again.
