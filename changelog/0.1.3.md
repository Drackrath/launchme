# launchme 0.1.3

Released 2026-10-26.

## New

- **Tarot** (Settings → System → Features → *Tarot — a reading of the cards*, off by default): a short card
  reading that ends with up to three games. `T` on the home screen, the pad's View / Create button or the card
  icon in the desktop window's top bar opens it.
  - The first card always asks whether to buy something new on Steam or play what you already own; then 4–7
    more questions, drawn at random: genre, era, played or not, length, mood, company, setting, art style,
    fame or a hidden gem, price (Steam) and whether the game should be installed (library).
  - Each answer is a tarot card with its arcana numeral, a drawn symbol, its name and meaning. The cards are
    dealt from the deck, turn face up, the chosen one rises and the others fall away; at the end up to three
    cards turn over to show the games' posters (Destiny, Temptation, The Wild Card).
  - Library readings use what the game index knows about your games (genres, tags, release year, reviews,
    length, play time). Steam readings ask Steam's public store search, filtered by the genre and price you
    picked, and leave out games you own; Enter opens the game's details or its store page.
  - Keyboard, pad and mouse; Backspace / B takes the last card back.

## Changes

- **Game knowledge fills in quietly**: after its first 240 lookups of a session the game index keeps going,
  one lookup every 15 seconds, until the whole library is described (it used to stop until the next start).
- **Game lengths in the background**: with the HowLongToBeat plugin on, owned games get their completion times
  one at a time (every 12 seconds at most, installed and recently played games first, never while a game
  runs), without opening Details. A run of lookups without results pauses it for ten minutes.
- Tarot's Steam searches are kept for 30 minutes (and an older copy is used when Steam can't be reached).
