# launchme 0.2.3

Released 2026-10-27.

## New

- **Publishers and developers** (Settings → Library → *Publishers and developers*, on by default): the games of
  the publishers and developers that have a logo (59 studios, from Capcom to Ubisoft), matched from what the
  stores and RAWG say of each game ("CAPCOM Co., Ltd.", "Ubisoft Montreal" and "Bandai Namco Entertainment" are
  Capcom, Ubisoft and Bandai Namco). Only studios with games in the library are shown; one without a logo is not.
  - **TV mode**: a *Publishers* row and a *Developers* row of their logos on the home screen. Their *All* tile
    opens a board of every logo; a logo opens that studio's games, with the other studios' logos as the tabs
    (`LB` / `RB`, `PgUp` / `PgDn`); Back returns to the board.
  - **Desktop window**: *Publishers* and *Developers* sidebar groups, folded until opened; a studio's section
    has its logo as the title. Right-click a studio for *Move up*, *Move down* and *Hide* (*Show all* in
    Settings brings hidden ones back).
  - In the details pane, *Developer* and *Publisher* are links: they open that studio's section, or search
    for it when it has no logo.
  - The search fields and `Ctrl+K` find games by their developer or publisher too ("devolver", "capcom"),
    with or without a logo.
- **Gold border**: a game with every achievement unlocked has a gold edge on its tile (TV mode) and its
  poster (desktop window).
- **Edit metadata in TV mode**: Details → "…" → *Edit metadata* opens the same dialog as the desktop window's:
  the D-pad moves between the fields and buttons, `A` types into a field with the on-screen keyboard, `B`
  closes it.

## Changes

- The publisher logos are HVR88's [Monochrome Gaming Logos](https://github.com/HVR88/Monochrome-Gaming-Logos),
  shipped in `assets\studios` with the author's note.
