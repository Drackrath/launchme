# launchme 0.4.4

Released 2026-10-30.

## New

- **Default library views in TV mode** (Settings → Interface → *Library views in TV mode*, at the bottom):
  *Default view of emulators*, *Default view of collections* and *Default view of publishers* (developers too)
  choose the view (Grid, List, Cinematic, Overview, Shelf…) a console's, a collection's or a publisher's library
  opens in until you pick one for it. Each has its own **Reset** badge (or Delete, or X on a pad): every library
  of that kind loses its own view, order and filter and follows the default again. The row says how many have a
  view of their own.
- **Delete an own collection from Settings** (Settings → Library → *Collections*): a trash after ↑ ↓ on each of
  your own collections, red under the pointer or with the focus (Right from ↓, or Delete, or X on a pad). It asks
  first: *Delete collection* (in red), *Hide on the home screen* (or *Show*), *Cancel* (focused). Steam's
  collections cannot be deleted from Launchme.
