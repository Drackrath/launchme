# launchme 0.2.2

Released 2026-10-27.

Suggested by frasersimpson126 ([#4](https://github.com/uxandai/launchme/issues/4)).

## New

- **Profile**: a page for the player, opened from the new *Profile* widget in the widgets drawer (`Tab` / Start,
  at the bottom; in the desktop window: the widgets icon, now first in the top bar). It shows the Steam name and
  picture, this week (play time against last week, games, launches, days played, an achievement streak, a chart
  of the seven days and the most played games), the latest achievements with their icons and rarity, the
  achievements of the last twelve months, the games closest to 100 % and every platform with its games, hours
  and account. A game opens its Details, a platform its library. Launchme keeps the week from the sessions it
  follows (`play-journal.tsv`) and the achievements it has seen (`achievement-history.json`); Steam's unlocks are
  read from the client's own files when the page opens. Not offered in the in-game Guide overlay.
- **MultiMC / Prism Launcher** as a PC platform (Settings → Library → PC platforms → *MultiMC / Prism*): every
  instance is a game, started with `--launch`, with the play time the launcher counted. Prism Launcher, PolyMC and
  MultiMC are found on their own; a portable MultiMC can be chosen in the platform's *Launcher* row.
- **Play time you set** (desktop window → *Edit metadata* → *Play time*): type `12`, `12,5` or `12:30` (hours);
  further play is counted on top of it, and an empty field goes back to the automatic value.
- **Epic play time**: with the Epic account connected, games show the play time Epic counted (when it is more
  than Launchme's own).

## Changes

- **Launch screen**: it stays until the game's window shows (the zoom slows to a rest) and fades away behind the
  game, instead of ending after a fixed few seconds.
- The desktop window's widgets icon opens the widgets drawer itself, like `Tab` in TV mode.
- Non-Steam games show the larger of Launchme's play time and the store's (Epic, Prism).
