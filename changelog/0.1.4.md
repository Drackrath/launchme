# launchme 0.1.4

Released 2026-10-26.

## New

- **Where the companions live** (Settings → Assistant → Where they live; by default each stays in her own city).
  The companions live in Europe and Tokyo and sleep at night by their own clocks, so players in other time
  zones often found them asleep. Now they can move:
  - *Shared city*: one of 27 cities from Honolulu to Auckland (Los Angeles, Vancouver, Denver, Mexico City,
    Chicago, Toronto, New York, São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Reykjavík, Lisbon, London, Paris, Berlin, Warsaw,
    Kraków, Istanbul, Dubai, Mumbai, Bangkok, Singapore, Seoul, Tokyo, Osaka, Sydney, …) for all of them.
  - *City: <name>*: the chosen companion's own city over the shared one, or *Her own city* to keep her home.
    Its description shows her local time there and whether she is asleep.
  - The city's time zone with its daylight saving (Europe, North America, Australia, New Zealand) sets her
    clock: when she sleeps, the time in her prompt and the real weather there. Her neighbourhood, street and
    flat are not written anywhere: the model picks them and keeps to them.

## Changes

- `dst=` in a companion's `character.txt` also takes `au` and `nz` (southern-hemisphere summer time).
