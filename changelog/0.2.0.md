# launchme 0.2.0

Released 2026-10-26.

## New

- **Videos in more places** (Settings → Media & data → *Background videos*): after the usual delay on a
  game, its video plays in List+'s picture, in Cinematic's focused landscape tile (instead of behind the
  whole screen) and, in the desktop window, in the details pane's picture.
- **Shelf: spines with the name** (Library → Shelf): the cases' left spines are black with the game's
  name printed along them, its size fitted to the spine. View options (Left in the first column, or the
  views button) add *Platform on the spine* (on by default: a band in the console's or store's colour with
  the platform's logo at the top of the spine) and *Grey cases at the sides* (on by default; off keeps
  every case in colour).
- **Shelf: the facts line stays**: under the title the platform's badge, then year · developer ·
  playtime, all appearing together once the game's details are in.

## Fixes

- The first move on a home screen (PSfive and the other layouts with the *grow* tile entrance, Settings → Interface → *Tile entrance*) made the row
  jump instead of gliding when it came while the tiles were still growing in.
- Shelf: the platform under the title showed for a moment and then disappeared.

## Changes

- **Landscape tiles fill their width** (Grid's landscape tiles, Cinematic, Overview): a narrower picture
  loses some of its top and bottom instead of showing empty bands at the sides.
- **Shelf cases are nearly square** and their artwork has square corners.
- **Long names in the List and Detailed views** fade out at the right instead of ending with an
  ellipsis; the focused one glides to its end and back.
