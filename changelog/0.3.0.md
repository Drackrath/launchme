# launchme 0.3.0

Released 2026-10-27.

## New

- **Hide to tray while playing** (Settings → Library → Launching → *Hide to tray while playing*, off by
  default): once a game started from launchme is running and in front, the launcher leaves the taskbar and
  Alt+Tab and waits as an icon in the notification area. It comes back when the game closes. Click the icon
  to show it, or right-click for *Show launchme* / *Close launchme*. Guide and the Jump List bring it back too.
- **Background crop**: in *Change artwork*, pick *Background* and then *Adjust crop* to choose which part of a
  wide background your screen shows. Move it with the arrows, the stick or the mouse, then *Save crop*.
  *Center* goes back to the middle. The crop belongs to that picture: a new background starts centred again.
- **Store art for every store**: when SteamGridDB has nothing for a game, launchme now uses the store's own
  pictures. Epic already did this; Ubisoft Connect, GOG, EA and Battle.net games now do too:
  - Ubisoft Connect: the box, background and logo the client keeps on this PC;
  - GOG: box art, background and logo from GOG's public product pages;
  - EA: pack art, key art and logo, fetched with the account import. Refresh the EA account once in
    Settings → Platforms → EA to get them;
  - Battle.net: key art and logo from Blizzard's public catalog.
- **Steam trailers for games from other stores** (Settings → Media & data → *Steam game trailers*): PC games
  from Epic, GOG, EA, Ubisoft, Battle.net or added by hand play the Steam trailer of the same game. The match
  comes from *Identify game* when there is one; otherwise the title must match a single Steam game exactly.
- **Companion: social dynamics**. The relationship (trust, respect, security, familiarity) now grows even with
  gamification off. Topics she was asked to drop stay dropped. She recalls the thread of a conversation, not
  only its last message. Corrected facts keep their history. Plans can be proposed, agreed, moved or turned
  down. Her *Mind* page lists what changed the relationship and why. Existing memories and settings are kept.

## Fixes

- **Sharp backgrounds on 16:9 screens.** Wide SteamGridDB backgrounds were reduced to 1920 px (2560 px in the
  cache) and then enlarged to fill a 1080p screen, so they looked soft. launchme now downloads the 3840×1240
  version when there is one and keeps enough of it to cover the screen. Backgrounds that the earlier cache
  compaction had shrunk are downloaded again once. Your own picks and crops stay. (Fixes #6)
- "No games in this view" is now centred on the screen in TV mode and in the desktop window.
- Steam trailers were not found with the interface in Portuguese or Spanish until the game's details had been
  opened in English.

## Changes

- *Change artwork* keeps every page you have opened until you close it. Going back from *Background* to
  *Poster* shows the poster page at once, without loading its thumbnails again.
